Amounts in USD millions, rounded to whole millions; EPS in USD per share. Latest filed versions, including later revisions. Select a value to see its SEC sources.

How to read this table
Fiscal year
Labels follow company reports. A fiscal year may end in the following calendar year.
Revenue
Total revenue as the company reports it; banks show net revenue. Definitions differ between industries, so compare revenue within similar companies.
Net income
Attributable to the parent, before preferred-shareholder and other per-share adjustments.
EPS
Diluted where available. Normalized for reviewed stock splits, so all years use the same share count basis; the sources show the originally reported value.
Parent equity
Belongs to the parent's owners at period end, including preferred equity and excluding noncontrolling interests.
Operating cash flow
Whole company, unless marked Continuing ops.
Basic
Basic EPS, because no diluted EPS is reported.
Continuing ops
Operating cash flow of continuing operations, excluding discontinued operations.
Split-adjusted
Reported EPS restated for stock splits after the filing.
Calculated
Calculated from values reported in one filing, not one reported figure.
Series break
This year and the year before come from different definitions, or a value has a different sign in the report, so the change between them in this table is misleading. Select the value to compare both years with the report that states them in one definition.
Needs review
An unverified definition or conflicting values; no value is shown.
Not available
No matching value was imported.
