Price ratios use the last close of each fiscal year. Values are as first reported in that year's annual report, so a year restated later can differ from the annual financials above.

How to read the ratios
Price
P/E, P/B and P/S use the last close on or before the fiscal year's end, at most 7 days earlier, in USD. Converted to the share basis of EPS, so it can differ from the price quoted on that day.
Values
As first reported in that year's annual report, with the year before as that report states it, so the price and the results describe the company in one shape. A later report can restate a year, for example without a business separated afterwards, while the year-end price still includes it; the annual financials table above shows such later revisions.
