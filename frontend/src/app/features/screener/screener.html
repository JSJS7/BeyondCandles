Sector

Ratios are not comparable across all businesses, such as banks and distributors, so compare similar companies (methodology (opens in a new tab)). Historical data for learning, not investment advice.

How to read this table
Price
Close of the valuation date, or the last close within 7 days before it, in USD. Converted to the share basis of EPS, so it can differ from the price quoted on that day.
Period end
End of the annual period used. Periods of different companies can differ by months.
P/E
Price ÷ EPS. Empty when EPS is not positive.
P/B
Price × shares ÷ common equity. Empty when common equity is not positive.
P/S
Price × shares ÷ revenue.
ROE
(Net income − preferred dividends) ÷ average common equity.
ROA
Net income ÷ average assets.
Net margin
Net income ÷ revenue.
Operating margin
Operating income ÷ revenue. Many companies, including banks, report no such row.
FCF margin
(Operating cash flow − capital expenditure) ÷ revenue.
Revenue, EPS growth
Later ÷ earlier − 1, both years from one report; otherwise empty.
OCF / net income
Operating cash flow ÷ net income. Empty when net income is not positive.
Equity / assets
Reported equity ÷ assets. Not a measure of debt.
Criteria
A range keeps the companies whose ratio lies inside it, limits included. A percentage is typed as a number of percent (15 means 15%). Companies with no value for a criterion are left out, because an empty value is not zero.
Median, 80% between
Under each criterion: half of the companies with a value are below the median. The 80% range leaves out the lowest 10% and the highest 10%, so a few extreme values do not hide where most companies lie. These are the 10th and 90th percentile, and each is the real value of one company.
Columns
Use the Columns button above the table to hide what you do not need. A criterion on a hidden column still applies, and the CSV keeps every column.
CSV
The companies as shown, in the shown order. Ratios are plain fractions (0.15 is 15%), and an empty field means no value, never zero.
—
No value: an input is missing or the result has no meaning. It never means zero. A ratio is also empty when what it divides by is not positive (EPS for P/E, revenue for P/S and the margins, net income for OCF / net income), when common equity is not positive (P/B; ROE in either year), when growth starts or ends at a value that is not positive or no report states both years, when only continuing operations' cash flow is reported (OCF / net income), and for price ratios without a comparable price (JCI until September 2016).
Basic
Basic EPS, because no diluted EPS is reported.
Calculated
Calculated from values reported in one filing, not one reported figure. Here it is the share count: (net income − preferred dividends) ÷ EPS.
Series break
The company's yearly series changes definition here, or a value changes sign. Growth still comes from one report that states both years, so it is comparable; the company page shows both versions.
Continuing ops
Operating cash flow of continuing operations, excluding discontinued operations.
Spin-off
The price no longer includes a business the company spun off after the period, while the period's results still do, so P/E, P/B and P/S can read too low until the next annual report.
